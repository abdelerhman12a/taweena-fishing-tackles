# CREDITS — TAWEENA scroll site

Structure and motion follow the BRIX 3D-scroll layout study (via the REA-Video
breakdown of the Abhay Tiwari reel) and the repo conventions of the owner's
`tidewright-fishing` project. No BRIX code, brand, copy or assets were copied —
every "BRIX" slot in the brief was replaced with Taweena's own material.

## Video & photos
- `assets/video/hero_master.mp4` and all derivatives (`hero_scrub.mp4`,
  `assets/hero/frames/*.webp`) — edited from the owner's uploaded Taweena film
  (tuna / angler / TAWEENA rod). Rhythm edit: segment speed-ups 1.25×–1.75×.
- `assets/photo/*.jpg` — still frames extracted from that same film.
  No external or stock photography is used anywhere on the site.

## Data
- Products, branches, phones, email: public Facebook page
